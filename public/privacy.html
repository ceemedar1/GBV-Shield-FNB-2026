GBV Shield Privacy Notice

Last updated: 12 October 2026

GBV Shield is a safety and support app developed for Resiliency Care Centre. This notice explains the information used by the Android and Huawei versions. For privacy requests, email info@resiliencycarecentre.org.za.

Information used and why

  • Account: The name, phone number, email address and password supplied at registration are used to create and maintain an account. Password authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication; the app does not display your password to other users.
  • Emergency contacts and SMS: Numbers you enter as trusted contacts are stored on your device. When you enable and trigger SOS, including a configured shake trigger, the app can send an SMS to those numbers. The SMS may contain your location, address, map link and a live-tracking link. SMS may incur carrier charges.
  • Location: With device permission, the app uses precise or approximate location to find nearby hospitals, police and support organisations, and to include your position in SOS. During a live-tracking session, updated coordinates are stored remotely and available to anyone who has the session link. A session is designed to end after up to eight hours or when stopped; network or device conditions can interrupt updates.
  • Stories and chat: Stories you choose to post and messages you send through app chat are stored to provide those features. Stories may be seen by other app users. Do not include identifying details in a story unless you are comfortable sharing them. AI-chat prompts and replies are processed to generate a response; do not rely on AI chat as an emergency service.
  • Technical identifiers: Account identifiers and authentication tokens are used to keep the services working and protect access. Some tokens are kept in the device's secure storage.

Providers and sharing

The app uses Google Firebase services for authentication, data storage, hosted live tracking and AI processing. Data may be processed outside South Africa. A person who receives your SOS or live-tracking link can see the location you share. We do not describe a live link as private to a named person: anyone with the link may be able to open it. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising in the app.

Control and retention

You can deny location permission, avoid posting a story, stop a live-tracking session, or stop using the app. These choices may limit safety features. Ending a session does not delete an SMS or link already received by someone else. Account, story and chat records may remain in the service until removed; an eight-hour live-link expiry is not an account-data deletion schedule.

Your privacy requests

To ask what information is held about you, request correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern, email info@resiliencycarecentre.org.za. Include enough information for us to identify your account, but do not email your password or a live-tracking link. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Safety note

GBV Shield is not a substitute for police, ambulance or other emergency services. SMS delivery, internet access, GPS accuracy and background operation are not guaranteed. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services directly when it is safe to do so.
